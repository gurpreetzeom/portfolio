    Product decisions behind the interaction

    Controls that make the review usable

    Each interaction illustrates a workflow decision, not just a screen state.

    01 · ownership

    Shared queue, clear permissions

    Reviewers act on assigned cases. A Reviewer Lead can see the wider queue, reassign or exit a review, and only edit a case when assigned to it.

    02 · traceability

    Decisions leave a record

    Checklist verification, assignment changes, queries, sendbacks and decisions appear with a demo identity and timestamp. Queries route to the last LE approver or RCM.

    03 · needs confirmation

    Keep open policy choices visible

    Sampling and time-based or manual bypass, handling of open queries at approval, and the Disbursement Maker gate for Legal Pending need business confirmation. This demo holds the case where a release rule is unresolved.
