Capri Global • Lending Platform
01

Reworking the Micro-LAP journey without weakening controls

Owned the end-to-end product lifecycle across Loan Origination and field PD journeys. The challenge was not simply digitisation; it was reducing friction while preserving credit, risk, legal, KYC/AML and disbursement controls across a multi-role workflow.

LOSMicro-LAPUnderwritingKYC / AMLWorkflow DesignPRDs
20–30%lower overall processing TAT
25–35%lower data errors & rework
30–40%less manual intervention
1,000+monthly loan journeys supported
Observe → map where cases stall, bounce or require manual checks
Design → rules, role gates, exception paths and user journeys
Align → credit, risk, legal, compliance, operations & engineering
Ship → tighter validation, workflow automation and clearer ownership
Measure → TAT, rework, error rate and operational adoption
Explore the thinking ＋

The product trade-off

Speed matters, but a faster journey that bypasses a credit or compliance control is not a better product. The design challenge was to make the right checks easier to complete and the next owner easier to identify.

What I focused on

Role gates, validation, exception paths and clearer ownership across the lending journey. I track turnaround time alongside rework and adoption so that a faster handoff does not simply move the problem downstream.

03 / Working principles

What I bring
to the room.

I started close to the data, then moved upstream into product. That path shaped how I work: understand the operating reality, quantify the problem, simplify the journey, define the controls, and stay close enough to engineering to ship the right thing.

01

Outcome over output

I care about TAT, conversion, contactability, data quality, cost and adoption — not feature count.

02

Controls can be UX

Regulated products do not need to feel bureaucratic. Good product design makes policy, risk and compliance easier to follow.

03

Data belongs in the loop

SQL, funnels, cohorts and root-cause analysis are part of product discovery for me, not a handoff to someone else.
