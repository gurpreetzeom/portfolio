Micro-LAP digital lending product management

I’m Gurpreet Singh, Senior Product Manager at Capri Global Capital. I work on digital lending journeys for Micro-LAP, including loan origination, field assessment, credit and operational workflows.

What is Micro-LAP?

Micro-LAP (often shortened to MLAP) is a loan-against-property lending context focused on smaller-ticket borrowers and businesses. Product design has to connect borrower, income, business, property and credit information while supporting the controls required by lending teams.

The product challenge

Micro-LAP cases can involve field evidence and multiple decision makers. A digital journey therefore needs more than a customer-facing application form: it needs clear ownership across sales, field teams, credit, risk, technical/legal processes, operations and disbursement.

Field assessment with Athena

Athena supports Personal Discussion field work including questionnaires, business and collateral photographs, income-assessment inputs and property information. The objective is to make information useful to downstream credit and operations teams while reducing avoidable re-entry and clarification.

From field visit into the LOS

In GAIA, the loan origination journey continues through review, approvals and disbursement.
